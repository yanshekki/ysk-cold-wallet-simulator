# CWS 1.1.2 (build 8)

YSK Limited · phone `hk.ysk.cws` · CWS Proxy `hk.ysk.cws.proxy`

Product: https://ysk.hk/products/ysk-cold-wallet-simulator

This note covers what landed after 1.1.1 (build 7): Cardano canonical signing (#73), Trezor and OneKey transport tests plus the static QR layout (#74), and the camera-preview settle (#75). It does not attach binaries. Signing identities and keystores are not in git. Seeds are not hardware-wallet safe.

## English

CWS is a phone simulator for developers and wallet teams testing interoperability with selected Ledger, Trezor, and OneKey Bluetooth models. It is not a secure element. Ledger, Trezor, and OneKey do not endorse it. Do not use it to hold real digital assets.

**Seeds are not hardware-wallet safe.** The public statement is [docs/security.md](https://github.com/yanshekki/ysk-cold-wallet-simulator/blob/main/docs/security.md). The 1.1.1 hot-wallet baseline still applies.

### What 1.1.2 changes

- Cardano `SIGN_TX` hashes the canonical transaction body. Catalog 8.x stays on the v8 machine. A host that sends v7 init (`P1` `0x01`) is handled as app 7.2.1 for versions 2.2 through 7.2.1. `GET_SERIAL` and `GET_APP_NAME` answer the public-key export probe. CIP-36 registration, pool registration, voting, and versions before 2.2 stay fail-closed. A submitted body that omits a withdrawal the website included is still a host mismatch.
- OneKey V2 replies that span 64-byte HID reports round-trip, including an ACK that shares a notify with the start of the next frame. Trezor USB HID and the local trezord bridge return `EthereumGetAddress` after `ButtonAck`. Those are transport tests. Trezor `906`, `471`/`472`, `1032`/`1033`, `1040`/`1041`, and OneKey `20106`/`20107`, `20120`, `10106`, Sui transaction ack, and TON proof stay KEEP.
- The static account PNG is ECC M, a four-module quiet zone, 8 px modules, and black on white. On 2026-10-31, `ki-mac-mini` decoded that PNG with local `zbarimg` only. Rabby, OneKey, and Keystone were not shown scanning it. That attempt is host-only KEEP.
- The phone QR preview waits until camera permission is granted, then settles, then mounts. Continuity Camera still unmounts on blur and remounts on focus.

## 繁體中文（香港）

CWS 是一款手機模擬器，供開發者及錢包團隊測試與指定 Ledger、Trezor 及 OneKey 藍牙型號的互通。它不是安全元件。Ledger、Trezor 及 OneKey 均未認可本軟件。請勿以本軟件保管真實數碼資產。

**種子不是硬體錢包級安全。** 公開說明見 [docs/security.md](https://github.com/yanshekki/ysk-cold-wallet-simulator/blob/main/docs/security.md)。1.1.1 的熱錢包基線仍然適用。

### 1.1.2 改變了甚麼

- Cardano `SIGN_TX` 對規範交易主體做雜湊。目錄 8.x 仍走 v8。主機若送出 v7 的 init（`P1` `0x01`），2.2 至 7.2.1 會按 7.2.1 的階段機處理。`GET_SERIAL` 與 `GET_APP_NAME` 回應匯出公鑰時的查詢。CIP-36 登記、權益池登記、投票，以及 2.2 之前的版本，仍然失敗關閉。若網站提交的主體含有提款，而送給手機的請求漏了該欄，那仍是主機不一致。
- 跨越 64-byte HID 報告的 OneKey V2 回覆可以往返，包括 ACK 與下一幀開頭共用同一個 notify 的情況。Trezor USB HID 與本機 trezord 在 `ButtonAck` 之後回傳 `EthereumGetAddress`。這些是傳輸測試。Trezor `906`、`471`／`472`、`1032`／`1033`、`1040`／`1041`，以及 OneKey `20106`／`20107`、`20120`、`10106`、Sui 交易確認與 TON proof，仍然是 KEEP。
- 靜態帳戶 PNG 是 ECC M、四模組安靜區、8 像素模組、黑白。2026-10-31 在 `ki-mac-mini` 只用本機 `zbarimg` 解出該圖。Rabby、OneKey 與 Keystone 沒有被示範掃到它。該次嘗試是只限主機的 KEEP。
- 手機 QR 預覽會等相機權限授予，再 settle，然後才掛上預覽。Continuity Camera 仍然在離開時卸載，回到前景時重新掛載。
